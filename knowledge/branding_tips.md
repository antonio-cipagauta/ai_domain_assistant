# Branding Tips for Premium Domains

A great domain is the first step in building a premium brand.

## 1. Easy to Pronounce
If you have to spell it out every time you say it, it's not a great branding choice.

## 2. Pass the "Radio Test"
If someone hears your domain on the radio, will they know how to type it into their browser correctly on the first try?

## 3. Avoid "Double Letters"
Domains like `bookkeeping.com` are harder to read and increase the chance of typos.

## 4. Evoke Emotion
Choose words that feel like your brand. `Swiftly.io` feels fast. `Anchor.com` feels stable.

## 5. Check Social Handles
Before you buy, make sure the matching handles are available on X (Twitter), LinkedIn, and Instagram.
