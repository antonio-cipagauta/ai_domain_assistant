# SEO Best Practices for Domain Names

Choosing an SEO-friendly domain name is crucial for long-term visibility.

## 1. Keep it Short and Simple
Shorter domains are easier to remember, type, and less likely to be misspelled. Aim for 6-14 characters if possible.

## 2. Keywords Matter
If your brand allows it, include a broad keyword related to your industry. For example, `techsolutions.com` vs `solutions.com`.

## 3. Avoid Hyphens and Numbers
Hyphens are often associated with spammy sites and are harder to communicate verbally. Numbers can be confusing (is it `3` or `three`?).

## 4. Brandable over Generic
A brandable name like `Google` or `Twitter` is better than a generic phrase like `best-search-engine-online.com`.

## 5. Stick with .com if Possible
Users still have a "type-in" bias for `.com`. If your target is local, consider country TLDs (.co.uk, .ca).
