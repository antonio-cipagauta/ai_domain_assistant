# TLD Guide: Choosing the Right Extension

The Top-Level Domain (TLD) tells a story about your brand.

## .com: The Gold Standard
Best for: All businesses, global reach.
Pros: Most trusted, easiest to remember.
Cons: Most expensive and hardest to find available names.

## .ai: Artificial Intelligence
Best for: AI startups, tech companies, data science projects.
Pros: High authority in the AI space, trendy.
Cons: Can be more expensive than .com.

## .io: Input/Output
Best for: Tech startups, SaaS, developer tools.
Pros: Very popular in the developer community, short and sleek.
Cons: Can feel too "techy" for non-technical audiences.

## .co: Company / Global
Best for: Startups and brands where the .com is taken.
Pros: Short, global, perceived as "Company".
Cons: Users often accidentally type .com instead.

## .app: Applications
Best for: Mobile or web application landing pages.
Pros: Very specific, modern, includes built-in HTTPS requirement.
Cons: Mostly restricted to apps.

## .net: Networking / Technical
Best for: Infrastructure, communities, and networking services.
Pros: Established and trustworthy alternative to .com.
Cons: Sometimes seen as "old-school".
